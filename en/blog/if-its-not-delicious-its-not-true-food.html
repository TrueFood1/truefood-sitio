If It's Not Delicious, It's Not True Food

Delicious, healthy and Gluten Free, in that order. Here's why our mantra starts with flavor and how it comes to life in every True Food product.

True Food was born with one idea we never let go of: make Gluten Free food people truly enjoy. Not the “good enough” version, not the one that just gets the job done. The one that sends you back to the kitchen for another.

When we sat down to define what we wanted the brand to be, three words came out, and they came out in an order we haven’t changed since: delicious, healthy and Gluten Free.

First, truly delicious

This is the rule that outranks every other rule, and it applies to everything that leaves our bakery. Before we think about ingredients, labels or seals, we ask ourselves one question: is this truly delicious?

If the answer is “kind of,” it doesn’t leave the bakery. We taste, tweak and taste again until every product gets where it needs to be: bread that’s soft inside and crisp outside, buns that are fluffy and toast up beautifully, a pizza crust with a golden edge and a sturdy base you can load up however you like, and cookies fresh from the oven, golden outside and gooey in the middle. That’s why we say it plainly: if it’s not delicious, it’s not True Food.

Then, healthy

For us, healthy doesn’t mean eating less or settling for boring food. It means caring about what goes inside. And here’s something that matters a lot to us: the ingredients we leave out are just as important as the ones we use.

We choose quality ingredients, the best we can find, and skip whatever doesn’t add anything. Our products have no artificial preservatives and no additives to stretch their shelf life. Instead, we freeze them: the cold does that job, and the product waits in your freezer, ready whenever you want to bake it.

In the end, it’s about eating well and feeling good, without having to choose one over the other.

And also, Gluten Free

All of our products are Gluten Free, and not because it’s trendy. It’s a conscious decision we made from day one, for people who need it and for people who simply want to feel good about what they eat.

And because it’s a decision we take seriously, we back it up: we hold GFFP certification, a seal that verifies that what we say on the package holds true in every product. So you can put True Food on the table with complete confidence, and nobody gets left eyeing the bread from a distance.

Why the order matters

If something doesn’t taste good, nothing else matters: nobody goes back for a second slice of toast because of the label. That’s why flavor comes first, quality comes right along with it, and Gluten Free comes built in, without costing you anything in taste.

That order is our filter for everything: every new recipe, every adjustment in the bakery, every product we decide to launch.

What the mantra looks like at home

It looks like White Bread toast with butter melting into it on an ordinary Monday. Seeds Bread with avocado. French Bread turned into bruschetta for a dinner that looks a lot more elaborate than it was. Buns toasted just right for Friday burger night. A Pizza Crust that goes from freezer to oven when everyone gets hungry at the same time. And a tub of Cookie Dough to bake when a sweet craving shows up out of nowhere.

Since everything lives in the freezer, it’s ready whenever the craving hits, not just on grocery day.

Find us in the freezers at Auto Mercado, Walmart, Más x Menos, MegaSuper, BM and Compre Bien. Try it and let us know. You already know the rule: if it’s not delicious, it’s not True Food.
