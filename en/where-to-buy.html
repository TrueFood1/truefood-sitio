At the supermarket, look in the freezer.

Our breads, Pizza Crust and Cookie Dough are sold frozen: you’ll find them in the freezer aisle, not on the bread shelf.

Why frozen
They have no artificial preservatives and no additives to stretch their shelf life. The cold does that job.
How to store them
Frozen at −18 °C. Shelf life of 9 months.
How to prepare them
Heat from frozen. They’re ready whenever the craving hits, not just on grocery day.
What to look for on the bag
The GF Certified Gluten Free seal: a third party, the Gluten-Free Food Program, verifies that what the package says is true.
